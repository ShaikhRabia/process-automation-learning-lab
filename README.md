# Process Automation Learning Lab

I enjoy taking messy processes, organizing information, and using technology to make work easier for people. This repository documents my learning journey in process automation, from analyzing and improving business workflows to building low-code automations and exploring how generative AI and AI agents can support them.

## Current focus

I am beginning with UiPath's Automation Business Analyst Associate Training to strengthen my skills in:

- Identifying and evaluating automation opportunities
- Understanding, analyzing, and documenting business processes
- Gathering requirements and considering exceptions
- Learning automation implementation methodology
- Exploring how agentic automation and human oversight fit into business workflows

## Repository structure

- **learning/** — Course notes, reflections, and learning evidence organized by platform
- **projects/** — Hands-on automation projects will be added as they are developed

## Learning approach

This repository reflects an active learning process. Entries will distinguish between course-provided material, my own notes and reflections, and any workflows or projects I build or customize.
